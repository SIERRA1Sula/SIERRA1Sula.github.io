02 · DESIGN PROJECT

PTO Tractor Gearbox

Design and analysis of a multi-speed gearbox for a tractor power take-off application.

Download Project Report ↓ All Projects

OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project focuses on the mechanical design of a tractor PTO gearbox. The design work considers the transmission arrangement and the selection and analysis of key mechanical components.

OBJECTIVES

Project Objectives

  • Develop a multi-speed PTO gearbox arrangement.
  • Design and assess the gears and shafts.
  • Consider bearing selection and gearbox housing material.
  • Evaluate design choices against the intended PTO operating requirements.

METHODOLOGY

Design & Methodology

  • Establish the transmission requirements and gear arrangement.
  • Carry out gear and shaft design calculations.
  • Consider bearing selection and component integration.
  • Select a suitable gearbox housing material.

OUTCOMES

Results & Outcomes

  • A five-gear arrangement was considered, providing four forward ratios and reverse.
  • Ductile cast iron ASTM A536 Grade 60-40-18 was selected for the gearbox housing.
